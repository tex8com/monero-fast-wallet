You choose

Fast Wallet is an extra, not a replacement.

Your normal wallets remain local. If you opt in, the app creates a separate receiving wallet and sends only its private viewing key to the Fast Wallet service.

  • No seed or private spend key is uploaded.
  • The Fast Wallet is clearly marked everywhere in the app.
  • You can choose Privacy only at setup or change the mode later.
  • For maximum control, the open-source services can run on your own node.

Straight answers

Questions people actually ask.

Is this a real Monero wallet?

Yes. Wallet data and synchronization come from the native Monero wallet core. The interface is made simpler, but the underlying wallet is not replaced by a website or exchange account.

Do I need Fast Wallet?

No. Choose Privacy only and use local wallets without the hosted Fast Wallet. Privacy + comfort adds a separate optional receiving wallet for faster alerts.

Does TEX8 hold my Monero?

No. The service never needs your seed or private spend key. A Fast Wallet can share only its separate private viewing key after you explicitly opt in.

What happens if I use Ledger?

Your spend key remains on Ledger and signing requires the device. You may separately approve secure local storage of the viewing key so balances can sync without keeping Ledger attached.

Why publish benchmarks?

“Fast” should be measurable. We publish the test contract, exact heights, resource costs, rejected runs, and limitations so the result can be reviewed instead of merely advertised.
